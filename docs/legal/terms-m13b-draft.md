# DRAFT — Terms of Service changes for RSVP invitation emails (M13B)

**Status: proposal for review. Not published.** `src/app/terms/page.tsx` is unchanged. These changes have not
been reviewed by a lawyer.

**Are changes needed?** Not strictly. Section 3 already makes organizers responsible for having authority to
record player information, and section 5 already forbids spam. But email invitations let organizers send
email to people who may not have an account, and those people are sometimes parents or guardians. Small,
explicit additions make the organizer's responsibility and the limits of the confirmation checkbox clear. We
recommend adopting them together with the Privacy Policy changes.

---

## Section 3 — "Organizers and players"

**Add after the Organizers bullet:**

> - **Contact emails and RSVP invitations.** Organizers may add a contact email for a player: the player's own,
>   or a parent's or guardian's. They may send RSVP invitation emails about their matches. Organizers must:
>   - only add addresses they are allowed to use for match coordination;
>   - keep them accurate, and remove an address when asked;
>   - send invitations only about real matches of their group.
>
>   The confirmation an organizer gives when saving a contact is their own statement. We don't verify it, and
>   it doesn't replace any permission the law or a parent / guardian requires.

**Current (Youth players bullet), unchanged except one sentence appended:**

> … including for showing youth players' names through Public or link-shared group pages and connected
> Telegram groups. **This includes permission to email a parent or guardian about their child's matches.**

## Section 4 — "Invitations, links and rosters"

**Add:**

> - RSVP invitation emails contain a personal answer link for one player. Anyone with that email can answer for
>   that player, so recipients shouldn't forward it. Opening a link never records an answer.
> - Recipients can unsubscribe from an organization's invitations at any time. We don't send invitations to
>   addresses that unsubscribed, bounced or reported spam, and organizers can't override this.
> - Each plan includes a daily and monthly allowance of invitation emails (counted in UTC). Sending may be
>   refused once the allowance is used.

## Section 5 — "Acceptable use"

**Current:**

> - send spam or unwanted messages through the service or its Telegram integration;

**Proposed:**

> - send spam or unwanted messages through the service, its invitation emails or its Telegram integration, or
>   add email addresses you aren't allowed to use;

## Section 11 — "Availability and disclaimers"

**Add:**

> - Email delivery depends on the recipient's mail provider. An invitation shown as "accepted" was accepted by
>   our email provider; that doesn't mean it reached the inbox. We don't guarantee that invitations arrive or
>   arrive on time.
